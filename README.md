# 🌌 Stranger Things

**Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery  
**Created by:** The Duffer Brothers  

---

## 🧒 Main Characters
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** – Loyal and brave friend  
- **Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo)** – Funny and clever  
- **Lucas Sinclair (Caleb McLaughlin)** – Cautious yet brave  
- **Will Byers (Noah Schnapp)** – The boy who vanished  

---

## 🎬 Plot Summary
> Set in the 1980s, *Stranger Things* follows a group of kids who uncover government secrets and supernatural events in their small town after their friend disappears. They soon encounter a mysterious girl named Eleven who holds the key to another dimension — **the Upside Down**.

---

## 💭 Why I Love It
- The nostalgic **80s vibe**  
- The strong **friendship and teamwork**  
- The balance between **horror and heartwarming moments**
